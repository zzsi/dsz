# Resources for Companies Expanding Overseas

This document consolidates publicly available resources that support organizations seeking to enter foreign markets. It includes government programs, international organizations, market research tools, financial assistance, legal guidance and networking support. Links were valid at the time of writing.

## U.S. Government Programs and Agencies
- [International Trade Administration (ITA)](https://www.trade.gov/) – Market intelligence, trade counseling and matchmaking services.
- [U.S. Commercial Service](https://www.trade.gov/us-commercial-service) – Export assistance through local offices and embassies worldwide.
- [Export.gov](https://www.export.gov/) – Guides, training and step-by-step exporting help.
- [Small Business Administration – Office of International Trade](https://www.sba.gov/international-trade) – Export counseling, training and the State Trade Expansion Program (STEP).
- [Export-Import Bank of the United States (EXIM)](https://www.exim.gov/) – Export credit insurance, working-capital guarantees and financing.
- [U.S. Department of State – Country Commercial Guides](https://www.trade.gov/ccg-landing-page) – Market conditions, opportunities, regulations and business travel guides for over 150 countries.
- [USA Trade Online (U.S. Census Bureau)](https://usatrade.census.gov/) – Official U.S. trade statistics.
- [U.S. Trade and Development Agency (USTDA)](https://www.ustda.gov/) – Project preparation assistance and partnership building in emerging markets.

## Government Support Outside the U.S.
- [UK Department for Business & Trade – GREAT](https://www.great.gov.uk/) – Export advice, market guides and trade missions.
- [UK Export Finance (UKEF)](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/uk-export-finance) – Government-backed loans, insurance and guarantees.
- [Enterprise Europe Network](https://een.ec.europa.eu/) – EU-wide network providing advice and partnerships for SMEs.
- [Access2Markets (European Commission)](https://trade.ec.europa.eu/access-to-markets/) – Tariffs, rules of origin and trade requirements for the EU and partner countries.
- [Canadian Trade Commissioner Service](https://www.tradecommissioner.gc.ca/) – Market intelligence and on-the-ground support for Canadian companies.
- [Export Development Canada (EDC)](https://www.edc.ca/) – Financing, insurance and bonding solutions.
- [Business Development Bank of Canada (BDC)](https://www.bdc.ca/) – Advisory services and financing for Canadian SMEs.
- [Austrade](https://www.austrade.gov.au/) – Government trade and investment development agency of Australia.
- [Export Finance Australia](https://www.exportfinance.gov.au/) – Government-backed loans, guarantees and insurance for Australian exporters.
- [Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade – Free Trade Agreement Portal](https://www.ftaportal.dfat.gov.au/) – Searchable database of Australia's FTAs and tariff schedules.
- [New Zealand Trade and Enterprise (NZTE)](https://www.nzte.govt.nz/) – Export development programs and global network support.
- [Japan External Trade Organization (JETRO)](https://www.jetro.go.jp/en/) – Investment and export promotion for Japanese companies.
- [Dubai Exports](https://www.dubaiexports.gov.ae/) – Export development and trade promotion for UAE companies.
- [Saudi Exports Development Authority](https://www.saudiexports.sa/) – Market intelligence, training and buyer–seller matchmaking for Saudi firms.
- [Qatar Development Bank – Export Development](https://www.qdb.qa/en/export) – Trade finance and export promotion services.
- [African Export-Import Bank (Afreximbank)](https://www.afreximbank.com/) – Trade finance, guarantees and advisory services across Africa.
- [Trade & Investment South Africa (TISA)](https://www.thedtic.gov.za/trade-division/tisa/) – Export promotion and assistance for South African companies.
- [Kenya Export Promotion and Branding Agency](https://www.brand.ke/) – Market research and trade promotion for Kenyan exporters.
- [Brazilian Trade and Investment Promotion Agency (ApexBrasil)](https://www.apexbrasil.com.br/en) – Export promotion and investment attraction for Brazil.
- [ProChile](https://www.prochile.gob.cl/) – Export promotion and market intelligence for Chilean companies.
- [ProColombia](https://www.procolombia.co/en) – Export, tourism and investment promotion for Colombia.

## International Organizations and Databases
- [International Trade Centre (ITC)](https://www.intracen.org/) – Trade Map, Market Access Map and SME trade tools.
- [Global Trade Helpdesk](https://globaltradehelpdesk.org/) – Joint ITC/UNCTAD/WTO portal combining trade statistics, market requirements and opportunities.
- [United Nations Comtrade Database](https://comtradeplus.un.org/) – Detailed global import/export statistics.
- [World Bank Open Data](https://data.worldbank.org/) – Economic indicators and country profiles.
- [World Bank Doing Business Archives](https://www.doingbusiness.org/) – Historical ease-of-doing-business rankings and regulatory information.
- [OECD Statistics](https://stats.oecd.org/) – International trade and economic data.
- [World Trade Organization (WTO)](https://www.wto.org/) – Trade agreements, tariff data and dispute information.
- [International Chamber of Commerce (ICC)](https://iccwbo.org/) – Incoterms, trade finance standards and global policy advocacy.

## Market Research and Country Data
- [CIA World Factbook](https://www.cia.gov/the-world-factbook/) – Country backgrounds, economies and infrastructure.
- [Euromonitor International](https://www.euromonitor.com/) – Fee-based market research and consumer data.
- [Statista](https://www.statista.com/) – Market and consumer statistics.
- [IBISWorld](https://www.ibisworld.com/) – Industry research reports.
- [UNCTADstat](https://unctadstat.unctad.org/) – Development statistics and foreign direct investment data.

## Export Financing and Insurance
- [International Finance Corporation (IFC)](https://www.ifc.org/) – Investment and advisory services in emerging markets.
- [European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (EBRD)](https://www.ebrd.com/) – Project financing and business advice.
- [World Bank – Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency (MIGA)](https://www.miga.org/) – Political risk insurance and credit enhancement.
- [Coface](https://www.coface.com/) – Private export credit insurance and risk assessments.
- [Euler Hermes (Allianz Trade)](https://www.eulerhermes.com/) – Trade credit insurance and bonding.

## Legal and Regulatory Guidance
- [Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS)](https://www.bis.doc.gov/) – U.S. export control regulations (EAR).
- [U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP)](https://www.cbp.gov/) – Import/export requirements and harmonized tariff schedule.
- [European Union Customs](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/customs-4/online-services/eu-customs-trader-portal_en) – Customs procedures and tariff classifications.
- [Food and Agriculture Organization – Codex Alimentarius](https://www.fao.org/fao-who-codexalimentarius/) – International food standards and safety guidelines.
- [International Organization for Standardization (ISO)](https://www.iso.org/home.html) – Global standards across industries.

## Networking and Trade Events
- [Global Entrepreneurship Network (GEN)](https://www.genglobal.org/) – Startup and SME community with global events.
- [Trade Events Search (ITA)](https://www.trade.gov/trade-events-search) – Directory of trade missions and shows.
- [World Economic Forum Strategic Intelligence](https://intelligence.weforum.org/) – Insights on global competitiveness and industry trends.
- [DHL Global Connectedness Index](https://www.dhl.com/global-en/dhl-global-connectedness-index.html) – Analysis of globalization trends.
- [Chambers of Commerce Directory (ICC)](https://iccwbo.org/global-icc/icc-membership/icc-national-committees/) – Contact information for national chambers worldwide.
